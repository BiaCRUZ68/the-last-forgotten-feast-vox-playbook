# Final Quality-Control Checklist

## 1. Historical accuracy and sourcing
- [ ] Every consequential factual claim in narration has a claim ID.
- [ ] Claim ledger includes real sources and exact locators where available.
- [ ] Primary evidence and scholarly interpretations are distinguished.
- [ ] Dates, quantities, place names, ingredient names, and historical terms are checked.
- [ ] Direct quotations have been verified against the actual source.
- [ ] No citation, archival reference, measurement, or quotation was invented.
- [ ] Disputed or uncertain claims are carefully qualified.
- [ ] Causal claims are not stronger than the supporting evidence.
- [ ] Reconstruction details are marked as reconstruction when appropriate.
- [ ] Historical recipe gaps have not been silently filled with invented ingredients or quantities.

## 2. Script and retention
- [ ] The opening raises a specific, accurate question.
- [ ] The episode answers the question posed in the opening.
- [ ] Each section adds information or changes the interpretation.
- [ ] Food remains central to the story.
- [ ] Explanations are understandable without prior specialist knowledge.
- [ ] Unfamiliar terms are explained when first introduced.
- [ ] Repetition, filler, and unsupported superlatives have been removed.
- [ ] The complete narration has been read aloud and timed.
- [ ] The ending gives a meaningful resolution without a generic moral.

## 3. Visual accuracy and clarity
- [ ] Every shot has a clear narrative purpose and narration cue.
- [ ] Visuals match the period, geography, ingredients, tools, and materials.
- [ ] Maps, timelines, labels, and diagrams are accurate and legible.
- [ ] AI-generated text has been checked or replaced with editor-added typography.
- [ ] Visuals do not imply a disputed claim is proven.
- [ ] Repeated objects, food, colors, maps, and locations remain consistent.
- [ ] Image-to-video motion is controlled and does not introduce new objects or distort details.
- [ ] Visual references and any archival material have suitable rights/permissions.
- [ ] The visual identity is original and does not misrepresent affiliation with another channel.

## 4. Audio and ASMR
- [ ] Narration is intelligible on speakers and headphones.
- [ ] ASMR/foley supports the image and does not compete with speech.
- [ ] Silence and dynamic contrast are used intentionally.
- [ ] Sound effects do not claim to be authentic historical recordings unless they truly are.
- [ ] Speculative sound recreations are documented as such.
- [ ] Music and SFX licenses cover the intended use.
- [ ] No clipped peaks, distracting noise, abrupt cutoffs, or unintentional jumps remain.
- [ ] Stereo image and mono compatibility are acceptable.

## 5. Packaging and export
- [ ] Title accurately reflects the story and evidence.
- [ ] Thumbnail communicates one clear idea at small size.
- [ ] Thumbnail text is brief, readable, and not misleading.
- [ ] Description does not introduce unsupported claims.
- [ ] Final export has the correct aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, and audio settings.
- [ ] Opening, ending, subtitles/captions, and any on-screen citations are checked.
- [ ] Final file has been watched from beginning to end after export.

## 6. Final sign-off
- [ ] Outstanding uncertainties are recorded.
- [ ] No major factual, rights, or continuity issue remains unresolved.
